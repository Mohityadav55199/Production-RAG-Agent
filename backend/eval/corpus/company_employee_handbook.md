# Company Employee Handbook

## Working Hours and Remote Work Policy
We are a remote-first engineering organization operating across distributed time zones:
- Core collaboration hours are 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM Eastern Standard Time (EST). Engineers should be available on Slack during this window for team standups and architectural syncs.
- Flexible scheduling allows team members to distribute their remaining 3 hours per workday according to personal preference.

## Paid Time Off (PTO) and Leaves
- **Vacation Allowance**: Full-time employees receive 25 days of accrued paid time off per calendar year.
- **Sick Leave**: 10 dedicated sick leave days are provided separately from vacation allowance and do not require advance manager notice.
- **Parental Leave**: 16 weeks of fully paid leave for primary and secondary caregivers following birth, adoption, or foster placement.
- **Public Holidays**: Employees observe 11 company-wide official holidays alongside 2 floating personal cultural holidays.

## Equipment Stipend and Home Office
- Every new engineer receives a $2,500 one-time hardware budget upon joining to acquire a laptop, ergonomic chair, or monitor.
- A recurring monthly remote stipend of $150 covers broadband internet and co-working space subscriptions.
- Hardware refresh cycles occur every 3 years.

## Engineering Standards and Code Reviews
- All production code requires a minimum of one approving review from a peer engineer before merging into `main`.
- Unit test coverage for new backend services must meet or exceed 80%.
- Sensitive secrets and API tokens must never be committed to git repositories; pre-commit git hooks block secrets before committing.
